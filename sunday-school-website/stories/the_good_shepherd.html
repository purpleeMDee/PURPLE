The Good Shepherd

Jesus told a story to help people understand his love. He said, "I am the good shepherd. The good shepherd lays down his life for the sheep."

Imagine a shepherd with a big flock of fluffy sheep. He knows each one by name. He leads them to green pastures to eat and to quiet waters to drink. He protects them from danger, like hungry wolves.

If one little sheep wanders off and gets lost, the good shepherd doesn't just forget about it. He leaves the other ninety-nine sheep in a safe place and goes searching for the one lost sheep. He looks high and low, over hills and through valleys, until he finds it.

When he finds the lost sheep, he is so happy! He gently picks it up, puts it on his shoulders, and carries it back to the flock. He calls his friends and neighbors and says, "Rejoice with me; I have found my lost sheep!"

Jesus explained that we are like his sheep. He loves us so much that he would do anything to keep us safe. When we feel lost or scared, we can remember that Jesus, our Good Shepherd, is always looking for us, ready to welcome us back with open arms.
